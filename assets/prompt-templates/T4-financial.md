# T4 — Financial

**Use when:** Cash flow, pricing, funding, cost control, profitability or cash forecasting.
**Level:** Basic or Moderate (the advisor decides depth)
**Fields marked with a bracket** are filled from what the user said. A field with no user data is removed and, if it matters, listed under *Not provided yet* (see `assets/output-format.md`).

## Template

```text
In my words:
"[...]"

Business:
- Type: [service-based | digital product/SaaS | physical product/e-commerce | local brick-and-mortar]
- Industry: [...]
- Stage: [startup | growth | established]
- Jurisdiction: [country, state or province]   (keep when funding, tax or payroll is involved)

Known (please don't re-ask):
- [How long the problem has been happening]
- [What has been tried and what happened]

Money:
- Currency and basis: [currency; monthly or annual; cash or accrual if known]
- Cash balance now: [...]
- Revenue per period: [...]
- Expenses per period: [...]
- Net burn or runway, if known: [...]
- How customers pay, and how late: [upfront | net-30 | net-60 | subscription | installments; typical delay]
- Debt, loans or investor obligations: [...]
- Problem type: [cash flow | profitability | pricing | funding | cost overrun]
- What changed recently: [pricing, headcount, a new customer, a new cost, the season]

Not provided yet:
- [...]

Assumptions to verify (not confirmed):
- [...]

Research context (unverified unless sourced):
- [...]

Outcome I want:
[one of: explain why cash is tight and what to change first | project cash for the next [period] | decide between [funding options] | set or change a price for [offer] | find where margin goes]
```

## Rationale cue

Say which inputs were supplied and which stayed Not provided, and ask for one diagnosis or decision, not every possible fix.
