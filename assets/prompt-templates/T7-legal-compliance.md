# T7 — Legal and Compliance

**Use when:** Contract issues, regulatory compliance, intellectual property, legal risk or a business dispute.
**Level:** Basic or Moderate (the advisor decides depth)
**Fields marked with a bracket** are filled from what the user said. A field with no user data is removed and, if it matters, listed under *Not provided yet* (see `assets/output-format.md`).

## Template

```text
In my words:
"[...]"

Business:
- Type: [service-based | digital product/SaaS | physical product/e-commerce | local brick-and-mortar]
- Industry: [...]
- Jurisdiction: [country, state or province]   (always required)

Known (please don't re-ask):
- [How long the issue has existed]

The legal or compliance situation:
- Type of issue: [contract | IP | regulation | dispute | data privacy | other]
- Who is involved: [clients | suppliers | employees | regulators | partners]
- Current status: [active risk | ongoing dispute | preventive review | already happened]
- Deadline or notice: [date, if any]
- Lawyer already involved: [yes | no]

Documents or agreements involved:
[contracts, policies, agreements]

Not provided yet:
- [...]

Assumptions to verify (not confirmed):
- [...]

Research context (unverified unless sourced):
- [...]

Outcome I want:
Rank the main risks, tell me the protective steps to take now, and tell me when and what kind of lawyer to involve.
```

## Rationale cue

Jurisdiction is always present; carry status, deadline and documents so the advisor can rank risk and say when to involve a lawyer.
