# T2 — Operational

**Use when:** A day-to-day process or workflow that breaks, runs inefficiently or fails repeatedly.
**Level:** Basic or Moderate (the advisor decides depth)
**Fields marked with a bracket** are filled from what the user said. A field with no user data is removed and, if it matters, listed under *Not provided yet* (see `assets/output-format.md`).

## Template

```text
In my words:
"[...]"

Business:
- Type: [service-based | digital product/SaaS | physical product/e-commerce | local brick-and-mortar]
- Industry: [...]

Known (please don't re-ask):
- [How long it has been happening]
- [What has been tried and what happened]

The process:
- Where it breaks: [start | middle | end]
- People involved: [number and roles]
- Frequency: [one-off | repeating]
- How it works today, as best I can describe it: [...]
- Existing SOP or written process: [yes, ... | none]

Not provided yet:
- [...]

Assumptions to verify (not confirmed):
- [...]

Research context (unverified unless sourced):
- [...]

Outcome I want:
Locate where and why the process breaks, recommend the repair, and tell me how I will know it worked.
```

## Rationale cue

Diagnose the exact point of failure: carry where it breaks, the people and the frequency.
