# T10 — Growth

**Use when:** Growing what works: scaling, expansion, a new location or market, a launch that extends the current offer, or a bigger team or operation.
**Not this template:** a pivot away from the current business, or a general direction decision, is T3.
**Level:** Moderate (Complex only if the interaction test passes; then use T8)
**Fields marked with a bracket** are filled from what the user said. A field with no user data is removed and, if it matters, listed under *Not provided yet* (see `assets/output-format.md`).

## Template

```text
In my words:
"[...]"

Business:
- Type: [service-based | digital product/SaaS | physical product/e-commerce | local brick-and-mortar]
- Industry: [...]
- Stage: [early | growth | mature]
- Jurisdiction: [country, state or province]   (keep when entering a new country, hiring abroad or raising money)

Known (please don't re-ask):
- [What success looks like]

The growth move:
[new market | new product or service | more customers | new location | bigger team]: [description]

What works today:
[strengths and assets to build on]

What blocks growth:
[constraints, bottlenecks, unknowns]

Numbers:
- Revenue per period and currency: [...]
- Team size: [...]

Not provided yet:
- [...]

Assumptions to verify (not confirmed):
- [...]

Research context (unverified unless sourced):
- [...]

Constraints:
- Budget: [range]
- Team capacity: [who can be deployed]
- Timeline: [when growth must show results]

Outcome I want:
Tell me whether this is the right move now, the main risks, what must be in place first, and how I will know it is working.
```

## Rationale cue

Judge timing and readiness: carry the move, the strengths, the blockers and the resources.
