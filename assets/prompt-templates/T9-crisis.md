# T9 — Crisis

**Use when:** A hard clock threatens the business: a dated deadline or a runway measured in days or weeks, not months.
**Level:** Moderate for one problem, Complex when several problems interact
**Fields marked with a bracket** are filled from what the user said. A field with no user data is removed and, if it matters, listed under *Not provided yet* (see `assets/output-format.md`).

## Template

```text
In my words:
"[...]"

Business:
- Type: [service-based | digital product/SaaS | physical product/e-commerce | local brick-and-mortar]
- Industry: [...]
- Stage: [startup | growth | established | turnaround]
- Jurisdiction: [country, state or province]   (keep when the crisis is legal or regulatory)

Known (please don't re-ask):
- [What has already been decided]

The crisis:
- Type: [financial | operational | reputational | legal | loss of a key person or client]
- What happened and when: [...]
- Hard clock: [deadline, runway or notice, with date or days]
- Who already knows: [team | clients | public | regulators]

Resources right now:
- Cash runway: [days or weeks]
- Team available: [...]
- External support: [lawyer | accountant | PR | advisor | none]

Other problems in play and how they connect (if any):
- [...]

Not provided yet:
- [...]

Assumptions to verify (not confirmed):
- [...]

Research context (unverified unless sourced):
- [...]

Outcome I want:
First: the actions for the next 72 hours in order, and what not to do. Then: how to communicate with the people affected, and an outline for recovery once things are stable.
```

## Rationale cue

Lead with the clock, the resources and who already knows; order the outcome by time: act, communicate, recover.
