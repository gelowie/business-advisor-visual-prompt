# T6 — People

**Use when:** Team problems, hiring, culture, conflict, performance management or retention.
**Level:** Basic or Moderate (the advisor decides depth)
**Fields marked with a bracket** are filled from what the user said. A field with no user data is removed and, if it matters, listed under *Not provided yet* (see `assets/output-format.md`).

## Template

```text
In my words:
"[...]"

Business:
- Type: [service-based | digital product/SaaS | physical product/e-commerce | local brick-and-mortar]
- Industry: [...]
- Stage: [startup | growth | established]
- Jurisdiction: [country, state or province]   (keep when discipline, dismissal or pay is involved)

Known (please don't re-ask):
- [How long it has been happening]
- [Impact on the business: output, morale, revenue]

The people situation:
- Who is affected: [a specific person | a team | the whole company]
- Nature of the problem: [conflict | low performance | turnover | hiring | morale | culture]
- Team size: [number]

What has been said or done so far:
[conversations, warnings, policy changes]

Policies or contracts in place:
[employment contract, handbook, none]

Not provided yet:
- [...]

Assumptions to verify (not confirmed):
- [...]

Research context (unverified unless sourced):
- [...]

Outcome I want:
Find the likely cause, recommend a fair and defensible next step, and flag anything that needs a lawyer or an HR professional.
```

## Rationale cue

Name who is affected, what was already said and which contracts exist; jurisdiction is there because employment rules differ by place.
