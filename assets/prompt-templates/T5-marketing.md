# T5 — Marketing

**Use when:** Brand, lead generation, audience targeting, content strategy, low conversion or customer retention.
**Level:** Basic or Moderate (the advisor decides depth)
**Fields marked with a bracket** are filled from what the user said. A field with no user data is removed and, if it matters, listed under *Not provided yet* (see `assets/output-format.md`).

## Template

```text
In my words:
"[...]"

Business:
- Type: [service-based | digital product/SaaS | physical product/e-commerce | local brick-and-mortar]
- Industry: [...]

Known (please don't re-ask):
- [How long the symptom has lasted]

Marketing today:
- Target audience: [who the user is trying to reach]
- Channels in use: [...]
- Monthly marketing budget: [range]
- Conversion rate or lead volume: [if known]
- Main symptom: [low traffic | low leads | low conversion | wrong audience | weak brand | customers leaving]

What has been tried:
[approaches and why they did not work]

Not provided yet:
- [...]

Assumptions to verify (not confirmed):
- [...]

Research context (unverified unless sourced):
- [...]

Outcome I want:
Find why the current approach is not producing results and which one or two changes to make first. Tell me which metric will show it is working.
```

## Rationale cue

Look for the one weak link between audience, channel and message; carry audience, channels, budget and symptom.
