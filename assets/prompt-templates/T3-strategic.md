# T3 — Strategic

**Use when:** Choosing a strategic direction: a pivot away from what works now, a change of model or positioning, or another major either-or decision.
**Not this template:** scaling, expansion or a new market for what already works is T10.
**Level:** Moderate
**Fields marked with a bracket** are filled from what the user said. A field with no user data is removed and, if it matters, listed under *Not provided yet* (see `assets/output-format.md`).

## Template

```text
In my words:
"[...]"

Business:
- Type: [service-based | digital product/SaaS | physical product/e-commerce | local brick-and-mortar]
- Industry: [...]
- Stage: [early | growth | mature | turnaround]

Known (please don't re-ask):
- [How long the decision has been open]
- [What has already been decided or ruled out]

The decision:
[the decision or strategic challenge]

Options I am weighing:
- Option A: [...]
- Option B: [...]
- Option C: [... if any]

If I choose wrong or wait:
[what is at risk]

Who else has a say:
[...]

External pressure:
[market, competitors, investors, if any]

Not provided yet:
- [...]

Assumptions to verify (not confirmed):
- [...]

Research context (unverified unless sourced):
- [...]

Constraints:
- Budget: [range]
- Decision needed by: [date]
- Risks I already know: [...]

Outcome I want:
Compare the options against my constraints and recommend one, with the main reason and what would change that recommendation.
```

## Rationale cue

Carry the options, the cost of being wrong and the constraints, and ask for one recommendation with its reason.
