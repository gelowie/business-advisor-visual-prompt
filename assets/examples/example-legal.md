# Example — Legal and compliance problem (T7)

Illustrative: a fictional business. Research lines show the format only; in a real run a sourced figure carries a real source and date.

## What the user said

> a freelancer i hired built our logo and website and now says he owns it

## Intake

Provisional pick: T7 (ownership dispute). Missing and essential, in T7 priority order: jurisdiction, issue type and parties, status and deadline, documents and lawyer. One batch of four questions was asked.

Answers: United Kingdom (England); no signed contract, only emails; paid in full; he offered to license it back for a fee; the course launch is in about three weeks; no lawyer yet. The business sells online courses.

## Research

One query on ownership of work commissioned from freelancers, with no names or details in it. No figure or rule passed the filter as a dated, sourced statement, so one qualitative pattern is kept, marked for verification against the stated jurisdiction.

## Delivered output

## 📋 Business Visual Prompt

**Topic:** Ownership of a logo and website built by a freelancer
**Problem:** A freelancer paid in full with no signed contract claims ownership of the logo and website and offers to license them back.
**Task:** Rank the legal risks, say what to do now, and say when to involve a lawyer.
**Template:** T7 Legal and Compliance
**Target:** business-advisor-pro

### Assumptions to verify before copying

- The freelancer was engaged as an independent contractor, not as an employee. Ownership of the work can depend on this, and you did not say it directly.

### Prompt to send

```text
In my words:
"a freelancer i hired built our logo and website and now says he owns it"

Business:
- Type: digital product/SaaS
- Industry: online courses
- Jurisdiction: United Kingdom (England)

Known (please don't re-ask):
- No signed contract, only email exchanges
- The freelancer was paid in full
- He says he owns the work and offered to license it back for a fee
- No lawyer is involved yet
- The course launch is in about three weeks

Assumptions to verify (not confirmed):
- The freelancer was engaged as an independent contractor, not as an employee

Research context (unverified unless sourced):
- Pattern (unverified): ownership of commissioned work often depends on whether there is a written assignment, and rules differ by country and by contractor versus employee status. Verify for the stated jurisdiction.

Outcome I want:
Rank the main risks, tell me the protective steps to take now, and tell me when and what kind of lawyer to involve.
```

### Why this structure

Ownership answers depend on where the business operates, so jurisdiction is stated and the research line is marked for verification. The prompt records the missing contract, the payment status and the launch date so the advisor can rank risk and say when professional help is needed. The contractor status is flagged as an assumption because the advice depends on it.
